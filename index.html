About the Program

More than an internship.
This is a movement.

The Edwards Education Academy Corps Program is a youth leadership and entrepreneurship initiative under the Mirror Madness nonprofit, partnered with Chef Diva and Sisters & Me Catering Experience. Students don't just observe — they lead, they build, and they earn real income while developing skills that will carry them for life.

🏛️
Civil Leadership
Learn how to show up, communicate with confidence, and make an impact in your community.
🌍
Social Leadership
Build meaningful connections and lead with empathy and purpose.
📈
Economic Leadership
Real money skills, commission structures, and financial literacy through hands-on experience.
💡
Social Entrepreneurship
Learn to build businesses that serve communities — not just generate profit.
🍽️
Culinary Skills
Hands-on experience with a real catering brand, learning the business of food from the inside.
💰
Earn While You Learn
As a Chef Diva Community Ambassador, every order you bring in earns you real commission — same day pay.
The Partnership

Powered by Chef Diva 👑

EEA Corps students become official Chef Diva Community Ambassadors — promoting and selling real meals within their community and earning commission on every order they bring in. This is not pretend business. This is real entrepreneurship with real results.

Sisters & Me Catering Experience serves Dallas, Little Elm, Denton, and Carrollton with weekly menus, meal prep packages, and full catering services.

Ambassador Commission
Daily / Single Orders20%
Weekly Menu Orders20%
Meal Prep Packages10%
